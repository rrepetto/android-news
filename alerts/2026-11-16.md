# Android Security Watch — 2026-11-16 (UTC)

## ArcticRAT: VPN null-routing to impair Play Protect, hidden VNC, and Wireless ADB abuse

- **Alert ID:** arcticrat-zimperium-2026-11-12
- **Research date:** 2026-11-12
- **Alert date:** 2026-11-16 (UTC)
- **Priority:** High (fraud / device takeover)
- **Canonical source:** https://zimperium.com/blog/arcticrat-ai-powered-android-rat

### Technical summary
Zimperium zLabs analyzed 78 ArcticRAT samples (42 droppers, 36 payloads), 21 C2 IPs and 129 control commands. A sideloaded dropper impersonating popular apps requests VPN permission and uses `VpnService.Builder` to route both `0.0.0.0/0` and `::/0` into a tunnel with no upstream, temporarily blocking network connectivity during secondary-payload installation. Zimperium describes this as blinding Google Play Protect telemetry; it is **not** evidence of a universal Play Protect bypass. After Accessibility access, the RAT supports remote UI automation, MediaProjection/VirtualDisplay hidden VNC, black-screen overlays, credential phishing, SMS theft, and LLM-assisted prioritization of banking/crypto apps and OTP/transaction messages.

Recent variants also implement local Wireless ADB discovery via mDNS (`_adb-tls-pairing._tcp`), pairing with an attacker-provided six-digit code, and shell commands to toggle Accessibility services and alter lock-screen credentials. Pairing still requires the relevant debugging/pairing conditions and code; this is **not** a demonstrated arbitrary ADB authentication bypass.

### Anti-fraud significance
Combines user-authorized platform privileges (VPN, Accessibility, screen capture, potentially Wireless Debugging) into device takeover and financial-session abuse without requiring root. A black overlay can conceal on-device activity while remote operators navigate financial apps. Malware-side collection of wallpaper and device telemetry also attempts to distinguish real devices from sandboxes.

### Affected versions / patches
Wireless Debugging exists on Android 11+; no specific vulnerable Android build or security patch level is established for the campaign. Exploitation depends on installation and permission grants, not a newly disclosed Android OS CVE.

### Defensive action
- Treat suspicious Accessibility, active screen sharing/MediaProjection, obscured-window interactions, and unexpected VPN state during sensitive authorization as correlated risk signals, not definitive proof individually.
- Protect transaction confirmation UI from overlays where supported; require server-side risk checks and step-up authentication for anomalous transfers.
- Monitor for suspicious VPN setup followed by sideloading and abrupt connectivity loss; on managed devices restrict unauthorized VPN, sideloading, and Wireless Debugging.
- Consider incident response for devices with suspicious ADB pairing or changed lock credentials; avoid treating a clean Play Integrity verdict as proof that Accessibility-based takeover is absent.
- Use Zimperium's published IOC list for targeted threat hunting, accounting for infrastructure rotation.
