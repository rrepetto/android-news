# Android Security Watch — 2026-11-23 (UTC)

## Bazuka: screen-clone overlays and self-pairing Wireless ADB in modular Android fraud framework

- **Alert ID:** threatfabric-bazuka-2026-11-18
- **Research date:** 2026-11-18; **alert date:** 2026-11-23 (UTC)
- **Technical summary:** ThreatFabric analyzed Bazuka, a dropper/core plus 23 dynamically loaded DEX modules. It can capture the home or lock screen and present the image as an overlay to conceal activity underneath, record input, intercept SMS/2FA, remotely operate the device and relay NFC data. Its core uses Accessibility to navigate Developer options, enable Wireless Debugging, capture the pairing PIN and establish a TLS connection to the ADB daemon; it also attempts to disable Play Protect. An LLM-integrated operator panel helps prioritize victims. These are malware capabilities, **not** evidence of an Android ADB authentication bypass or a Play Integrity bypass.
- **Anti-fraud significance:** A convincing frozen-screen overlay can conceal unauthorized in-app operations, while Accessibility + local ADB pairing expands the ability to control the device and grant permissions. First-stage APKs can request minimal permissions and load additional modules later; app-manifest checks alone can miss the behavior. ADB-assisted persistence after host removal was discussed as a future risk, not established for Bazuka.
- **Affected versions / patches:** No Android CVE or security patch level was identified. Wireless Debugging is available on Android 11+; exploitation requires the relevant user/device permissions and conditions. ThreatFabric has **not** observed actual Google Play distribution, despite developer intent.
- **Defensive action:** Correlate unexpected Accessibility service enablement, Developer options/Wireless Debugging state changes, suspicious overlays and MediaProjection with high-risk transactions; enforce independent server-side transaction confirmation and risk controls. Hunt for dropper hashes `f046b50166c8229b49839eeebcc7fd14bf3f198e4ec1bfde8a5b271e1407dec5` and `4d572669d6000304a692874c1583021dfdb4f2f113ea487e470f7e4c486e503a` where device telemetry permits. Do not assume Play Integrity attestation rules out a compromised app environment.
